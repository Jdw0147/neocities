This is where I'll catalog all my book stuff! I got back into reading for fun in January of 2024 and since have come to tremendously cherish reading as a hobby. Finding some nice quiet time either outside or inside to read a chapter or two of a book is always a very good de-stressor and helps calm my brain down when it gets too overstimulated. Trying to read a minimum of three books a month but there are a lot of external factors that play into that such as the length of the book so I'm not too strict on myself.
