SONG OF THE DAY

This is a habit I picked up at the start of 2025. Picking a song to represent each day of that year. The song could have a deeper representation of a particular day or could just be a song attached to a core memory/a song I was really enjoying at the time. Regardless of reason this is a super fun little project to do and I encourage everyone to do it or something similar because it's so neat to go back and look at your year through music picked by you.
