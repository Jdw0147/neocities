Links & Resources

Useful tools / assets

  • Betty's Graphics - Cool free tiled backgrounds
  • CodePen - More cool free tiled backgrounds
  • Blinkie's Cafe - A cool custom blinkie maker
  • EzGif - Free online gif maker/editor. You can do a lot on here
  • 3D Gif Maker - What it sounds like. A free online 3d gif maker with a lot of cool options and presets
  • Dither It - free website you can use to dither images
  • Photopea - Free online photo editing software. Pretty much photoshop but a free browser tool
  • removebg - Free online tool to remove backgrounds from images. Works really well.
  • ribo.zone - massive free collection of really neat borders
  • broider - another collection of more classic borders, with the option to make your own. Very easy to use and useful
  • CoolText.com - Large library of free text presets to make flashy titles with.
  • hekate2 button maker - a free tool to make custom buttons (no gif support)
  • Ritual.sh 88x31 button creator - another free tool to create buttons with gif support
  • The 88x31 GIF Collection large collection of 88x31 buttons you can put on your site
  • Gif Cities - massive library of free old-internet gifs
  • Spriters - A large collection of free IP and unique sprites to litter around your site. Some really neat sprites on here
  • Sora's Pixel Converter - A pixel art converter. Has some neat effects too.
